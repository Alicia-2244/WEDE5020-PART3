About Us

Paw Haven Pet Care Centre is a caring and welcoming place where pets receive the attention, care and love they deserve.

We provide a safe and comfortable environment for pets while their owners are busy, travelling or away from home.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality pet care and create a safe, healthy and happy environment for every pet.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted pet care centre known for excellent service, kindness and responsible pet care.

  • Safe environment for pets
  • Professional pet care services
  • Comfortable facilities
